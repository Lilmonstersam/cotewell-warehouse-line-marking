Frequently asked questions

How long does an industrial floor coating last?

It depends far more on the system and the preparation than the brand of resin. Where a floor fails early it is almost always preparation, moisture or the wrong system for the traffic, which is why we test before we quote.

How much downtime should we plan for?

Quoted zone by zone before we start, and sequenced around your shifts.

Which finish suits my facility?

The standard system that suits most facilities is a three-coat system: primer, epoxy build coat and polyurethane top coat. We recommend a system based on your facility and the way your operations run.

What line marking options are available?

Options include line marking tape, single-pack paint, two-pack epoxy paint, cold-applied plastic and projected line marking. We recommend a solution based on your facility, surface and operating conditions.

Where does Cotewell work?

Cotewell services industrial sites across Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria, with Brisbane as the home base. Floor marking products ship Australia-wide from stock.

How much does epoxy flooring cost?

As a guide, the general range is $40/m² to $100/m², depending on the size of your site and the system used. A site assessment confirms the scope and price.
