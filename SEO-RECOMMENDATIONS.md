Research date: 7 September 2026  
Market: Australia  
Target URL: `https://cotewell.com.au/service/line-markings/`

These changes are proposed only. They have not been applied to the layout mock-up's metadata or primary heading.

## Recommended changes

### 1. Metadata and primary heading

Approve the following exact replacements:

- Title: `Warehouse & Commercial Line Marking | Cotewell Australia`
- Meta description: `Warehouse and commercial line marking for factories, workshops and industrial sites. Improve safety, traffic flow and organisation. Request a quote.`
- H1: `Warehouse & Commercial Line Marking Services`
- Canonical: retain the existing URL.

Rationale: the current title and H1, `Line Markings`, do not define the buyer, environment or service intent. The proposed wording covers the two agreed targets without making the title unreadable.

### 2. Replace the hero introduction

Proposed copy:

> Cotewell plans, supplies and installs warehouse and commercial line marking for factories, workshops and industrial sites across Australia. Create clear pedestrian walkways, forklift routes, loading zones and equipment areas using paint, epoxy, tape, cold applied plastic or projected markings selected for your floor and operations.

Keep the primary CTA as `Request a Quote`. Change the secondary CTA to `Compare line marking options`.

### 3. Make the page service-first

The current page mixes installation services and products before fully establishing Cotewell as the provider. Keep the first half of the page focused on:

1. The environments served.
2. What Cotewell plans and marks.
3. Material selection.
4. Site assessment and installation.
5. Project proof.

Move tape and projector product cards below the service, process and proof sections. This keeps product shoppers supported without diluting the contractor intent.

### 4. Add an intent-matched application section

Recommended H2: `Warehouse, Factory & Commercial Line Marking`

Recommended H3s:

- `Warehouse line marking`
- `Factory and manufacturing line marking`
- `Workshop floor line marking`
- `Distribution and logistics line marking`
- `Commercial car park and loading-area marking`, only if Cotewell actively delivers this work

Each application should name concrete outputs such as pedestrian walkways, forklift lanes, crossings, loading bays, exclusion zones, machine footprints, storage areas and safety symbols.

### 5. Strengthen the material comparison

Retain all five current options, but add buyer-decision fields:

- Suitable surfaces.
- Traffic and wear level.
- Cleaning and chemical exposure.
- Expected layout-change frequency.
- Indoor or outdoor suitability.
- Indicative access or cure requirements.
- Repair or replacement method.

The page should not publish lifespan, cure time or price claims until Cotewell has supplied verified ranges for each system.

### 6. Replace the blanket standards claim

Current copy says the markings adhere to Australian standards. This is too broad unless Cotewell can identify the exact standards, codes, test methods and limits that apply to each service.

Recommended interim wording:

> We plan line marking around your site layout, traffic management plan and operational safety requirements. Where a nominated standard or specification applies, confirm it with Cotewell during scoping.

Safe Work Australia's warehousing traffic-management guide supports clearly indicating traffic flow, parking areas, driver safety zones, pedestrian exclusion zones, keep-clear zones and speed limits. It also recommends line markings and marker posts at docks. WorkSafe Victoria includes floor markings as one control within a broader forklift traffic-management plan. The page should therefore present line marking as part of the control system, not as a standalone compliance guarantee.

### 7. Add proof that matches the buying decision

Retain the Tubeworx project and expand it only with verified information:

- Facility type and location.
- Marking method and substrate.
- Area or lineal metres.
- Installation or access window.
- Traffic and cleaning conditions.
- Measured or client-reported outcome.
- Dated project photography.

Avoid unsupported numeric claims. One properly evidenced project will be more persuasive than multiple generic project tiles.

### 8. Add buyer-focused FAQs

Recommended visible questions:

- `How much does warehouse line marking cost?`
- `Which line marking system is best for a warehouse floor?`
- `How long does warehouse line marking last?`
- `Can line marking be installed without shutting the whole warehouse?`
- `What should be included in a warehouse line marking plan?`
- `What are the Australian requirements for warehouse line marking?`

The first three questions should only receive numeric answers after Cotewell confirms defensible ranges. For standards, explain that requirements depend on the workplace, risk assessment, traffic-management plan and jurisdiction, then link to the relevant regulator guidance.

### 9. Add structured data after copy approval

Recommended graph:

- `Service` for warehouse and commercial line marking.
- `Organization` as the provider.
- `FAQPage` only for questions and answers that are visibly present on the page.
- `BreadcrumbList` matching the live hierarchy.

Do not use `Product` for the service itself. Keep product schema on the individual tape and projector product pages.

### 10. Improve media and internal linking

- Rename uploaded images descriptively, for example `warehouse-line-marking-pedestrian-forklift-zones.jpg`.
- Write alt text for what is visible, not as a keyword container.
- Link from the line marking tape category and relevant learning-centre articles to the service page with natural anchors such as `warehouse line marking service` or `commercial line marking installation`.
- Link from this page to floor coating, resurfacing, tape, projectors, the free sample form and relevant case studies.
- Add contextual links from verified warehouse and factory project pages back to the service page.

### 11. Handle location demand deliberately

Ahrefs reports meaningful local variants, including Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth. Cotewell's navigation currently states Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne.

Do not create thin, near-duplicate city pages. First strengthen this national service page and add genuine service-area proof. Create a city page only where Cotewell can provide unique local projects, delivery details, testimonials and operational information.
